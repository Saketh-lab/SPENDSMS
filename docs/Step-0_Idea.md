The idea is to build an Android-first mobile app that securely analyses a user-selected period of transactional SMS messages—such as the past month or year—to identify purchases, expenses and recurring subscriptions. It would extract details such as the amount, merchant and date, organise the transactions into categories, and present the results through a simple dashboard showing spending patterns, monthly totals and active subscriptions. The MVP would focus only on SMS-based transaction detection, basic categorisation and a clear spending and subscription overview.
